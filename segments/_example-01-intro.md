## Why this segment exists
This is the cold open and the show's intro. It must hook the listener
in the first 10 seconds, then settle into a quick "this is the show,
this is what's coming" beat.

## Structure
1. **Cold open (~10-15 seconds).** host_b teases the day's most
   striking item in one or two sentences. host_a reacts ("wait, what?"
   / "we'll get there"), then transitions into the formal show open.
2. **Show open (~20 seconds).** host_a greets the listener, names the
   show, says the date, and previews the rundown in one breath
   ("Today we've got AI news, the markets, what's happening in
   Washington, and the rapid round at the end."). Then hand off to
   host_b for the first real segment.

## Format / tone
- Length: ~30-45 seconds total
- Style: two-host conversational
- Tone: warm, fast — a friend telling you what they heard. Don't
  over-produce; don't sound like FM radio.

## Skip research
This segment doesn't need its own research call — the cold-open
content comes from the other segments' research briefs, which the
script writer sees when composing the show.
