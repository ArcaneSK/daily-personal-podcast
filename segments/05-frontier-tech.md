# Frontier Tech & Science

## Why this segment exists
Surface real, concrete advances in physical-world technology — not vapor,
not hype-cycle marketing. I want to know when something actually got
demonstrated, published peer-reviewed, shipped a commercial unit, or hit
a verified benchmark. The bar is "a thing exists or was measured," not
"a startup announced ambitions."

## What to cover

### Battery technology
- Cell chemistry breakthroughs with measured energy density / cycle life /
  cost numbers (lithium-iron-phosphate, sodium-ion, solid-state, Li-S,
  lithium-metal, anode-free)
- Solid-state battery progress from Toyota, QuantumScape, Solid Power,
  Factorial, Samsung SDI, CATL, BYD
- Manufacturing milestones (gigafactory commissioning, capacity adds)
- New battery research papers from MIT, Stanford, Argonne, NREL, Fraunhofer
- Grid-scale storage deployments and form-factor news (iron-air, vanadium
  flow, Form Energy, ESS, Hyme, etc.)

### Solar and renewables
- New cell efficiency records (NREL chart updates, perovskite-tandem)
- Heterojunction, TOPCon, back-contact module shipping news
- Floating solar, agrivoltaics, building-integrated PV demonstrations
- Major utility-scale projects breaking ground or coming online
- Solar manufacturing capacity (esp. U.S. domestic per IRA)

### Energy more broadly
- **Fusion:** Helion, Commonwealth Fusion, TAE, Tokamak Energy, NIF,
  ITER milestones — only when actual numbers (Q, fusion gain, plasma
  duration) are reported
- **Geothermal / next-gen geo:** Fervo, Eavor, AltaRock — drilling
  milestones, MW deployed
- **Nuclear:** SMR licensing (NuScale, X-energy, Oklo, TerraPower,
  BWXT), restarts of mothballed plants (Three Mile Island, Palisades,
  Duane Arnold), uranium supply news
- **Grid:** transmission projects approved, HVDC links, virtual power
  plants
- **Hydrogen:** electrolyzer cost / efficiency, green hydrogen projects
  reaching FID

### Biotechnology
- Major clinical-trial readouts (especially Phase 2/3 for first-in-class
  therapies)
- New CRISPR and gene-editing platforms, in-vivo delivery breakthroughs
- mRNA platform extensions beyond vaccines
- AI-for-biology models with measurable outcomes (AlphaFold 3, ESMFold,
  RFDiffusion, Chai-1, Boltz, OpenFold updates; protein-design wins)
- Synthetic biology platform companies shipping products (Ginkgo, Twist,
  Zymergen, etc.)
- Cell and gene therapy approvals, CAR-T progress, base/prime editing
  in clinical work

### Longevity, aging, and anti-aging
- Real research from labs (Sinclair lab, Buck Institute, Calico, Altos
  Labs, Retro Bio, Loyal, Rejuvenate Bio)
- Clinical trials for senolytics, rapamycin analogs, NAD+ pathway
  modulators, partial reprogramming
- Epigenetic aging clock validation, biomarkers of aging
- Caloric-restriction-mimetic research with measured outcomes
- Vet-medicine longevity products (often lead human trials)
- Skip the supplement-marketing layer — I want peer-reviewed data or
  registered clinical-trial readouts, not influencer claims

### Robotics
- Humanoid robotics milestones (Figure, 1X, Apptronik, Boston Dynamics,
  Tesla Optimus, Sanctuary AI, Agility, Unitree)
- Industrial automation deployments at scale (warehouses, manufacturing)
- Surgical and medical robotics (Intuitive, J&J, Medtronic, CMR Surgical)
- Autonomous vehicle real-world milestones (Waymo expansions, Zoox,
  Cruise, Tesla FSD measurable improvements)
- Drone / UAV technology with verified specs (BVLOS approvals, payload
  records)
- Open-source robotics releases (Hugging Face LeRobot, NVIDIA GR00T,
  PI's pi0)

## Sources to prioritize
**Concrete-news bias:** prefer sources that report what was measured or
shipped, not what was announced or promised.

**Primary sources**
- Nature, Science, Cell, NEJM (peer-reviewed)
- arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv (preprints — flag as such)
- Company press releases when they include actual numbers
- ClinicalTrials.gov, EMA, FDA databases for trial readouts and approvals
- NREL, DOE national labs (ORNL, Argonne, NREL, LBNL) for energy/
  materials

**Serious science press**
- Quanta Magazine
- MIT Technology Review (skip the trend pieces, take the news)
- IEEE Spectrum
- Ars Technica science section
- Heatmap News (energy / climate tech)
- Volts.wtf (David Roberts) for energy
- BloombergNEF, Canary Media, Latitude Media for energy markets and tech
- Construction Physics (Brian Potter) for built-environment tech
- Casey Handmer's blog for solar / industrial tech analysis
- Trevor Klee, Derek Lowe (In the Pipeline) for biotech
- Endpoints News, STAT News for biotech
- Levels Health blog, Peter Attia's content for longevity (filter for
  citations)

## What to skip
- Vaporware announcements ("we will build X by 2030") with no demo
- Crowdfunding campaign launches
- Hype-cycle pieces about industries that didn't actually do anything
  this week
- Influencer supplement claims, anti-aging marketing without trial data
- "Could this be the breakthrough?" framing — only "this thing was
  demonstrated/measured/approved" framing

## Format / tone
- Length: ~3-4 minutes spoken
- Style: two-host conversational. Aaron asks the curious "wait, that
  could actually work?" follow-ups. Emily delivers the substance and
  pushes back when something sounds promotional ("did they actually
  publish numbers, or is this a press release?").
- Tone: skeptical-but-curious. Engineering-grade specificity when the
  source provides it (energy density in Wh/kg, plasma confinement in
  seconds, trial endpoints with effect sizes).

## Recurring threads to watch
- Solid-state battery commercialization timeline (year-over-year)
- Fusion Q values and pilot-plant milestones (CFS SPARC, Helion 7, ITER)
- Humanoid robot ship-in-volume timeline
- Senolytics and partial-reprogramming clinical translation
- Geothermal scaling beyond pilot projects
- AI-for-biology measurable wins in drug pipelines
- SMR first-of-kind builds and licensing
