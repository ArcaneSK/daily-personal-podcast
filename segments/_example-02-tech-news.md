## Why this segment exists
Catch me up on what actually happened in tech in the last 24 hours.
I want signal, not hype. Concrete releases, papers, and developments
from real companies, not "AI is going to change everything" takes.

## What to cover
Cover anything notable in any of these areas. If multiple have news,
give the more impactful ones more time but don't drop the others.

### Frontier AI models
- Frontier model releases, benchmarks, and capability updates from
  the major labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Mistral)
- Notable open-weight releases (Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, etc.)
- Notable research papers when widely discussed

### Developer tools
- New AI-native IDEs and coding assistants
- Agent runtimes and orchestration frameworks
- Notable open-source releases on the day's GitHub trending list

### Hardware
- Chip announcements with real benchmarks
- New consumer hardware that meaningfully changes the form factor

## Sources to prioritize
- Lab blogs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta AI)
- Hacker News front page (filter for substance)
- The Information, Stratechery, Import AI newsletter
- Hugging Face trending models / spaces / papers
- arXiv preprints and Papers With Code when a paper is making rounds

## What to skip
- Generic "AI will transform X" hot takes
- Funding announcements unless > $100M or strategically interesting
- Twitter/X drama and personality news
- Repeated coverage of stories from yesterday unless there's a real
  update — check segment history

## Format / tone
- Length: ~5-7 minutes spoken (lean longer if there's a lot of real
  news, shorter if it's quiet)
- Style: two-host conversational. host_a asks the curious "wait, what
  does that mean?" follow-ups; host_b delivers the substance and
  pushes back when something is overhyped.
- Tone: technical-when-it-matters, no hype, but allow genuine
  excitement when a release is genuinely impressive.

## Recurring threads to watch
- Open-weight models reaching parity with frontier APIs
- AI agent ecosystems shipping production tools
- Real-world enterprise deployment signals (not announcements —
  actual usage)
