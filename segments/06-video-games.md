# Video Games

## Why this segment exists
Catch me up on video-game news that actually fits what I play. New
releases, upcoming announcements, demos, patches, deals, and behind-the-
scenes news for games that match my taste. Skip everything that doesn't.

## My genres (prioritize these)
- Story-driven action / cinematic games
- Puzzle + exploration ("figure it out" games)
- Weird, atmospheric sci-fi (light mystery, not horror)
- Open-world (but easy to jump into, low friction)
- Co-op action games (fun, not stressful)

## Genres / tags I actively don't want
Skip news for these unless it's a generational event (e.g., a new
Nintendo console launching). Even then, only headline-level mention.
- Heavy horror, jump scares, psychological horror
- Post-apocalyptic settings
- Games with steep re-learning curves (hardcore sims, roguelike
  permadeath, deep-systems strategy)
- Slow, lore-heavy starts (souls-likes that demand 10 hours before
  things click, unless they're widely loved by people who hate that)
- Anything that feels like work (extraction shooters with grind,
  gacha, pay-to-win, MMO time-gates)
- Sports sims (FIFA/Madden/NBA 2K) and racing sims unless something
  unusually noteworthy
- Battle royale / competitive PvP esports
- Most fighting games

## What to cover
On any given day, prioritize in this order:

### Releases and demos out today or this week
- New games launching that fit my genres
- Demos available on Steam Next Fest, Itch, console storefronts
- Free-to-keep games (Epic Games Store weekly, PlayStation Plus / Xbox
  Game Pass adds)

### Upcoming announcements with new info
- Reveal trailers for games matching my genres
- Release-date updates, gameplay deep dives, dev diaries
- State of Play / Xbox Showcase / Nintendo Direct / Summer Game Fest /
  Game Awards segments worth knowing about

### Major patches and content updates
- Substantial updates to games I might already own (DLC, free expansions,
  major patches that change the experience)

### Industry news that affects what gets made
- Studio acquisitions (Microsoft / Sony / Tencent / Embracer moves)
- Studio closures or layoffs at studios making games I'd play
- Notable studios spinning out (e.g., ex-Rockstar, ex-Naughty Dog
  forming new teams)
- Engine news that affects game tech (Unreal, Unity, Source 2, custom
  engines from FromSoft / Capcom / Insomniac etc.)

### Reviews and critical reception
- Notable review embargo lifts for games matching my genres — give me
  the OpenCritic / Metacritic average and the Eurogamer/RPS take
- Sleeper hits that scored well on Steam / OpenCritic that I might miss

### Hardware (lower priority, only when newsworthy)
- New consoles, Steam Deck and successors, ROG Ally / Legion Go / MSI Claw
  successors, VR headset news (Quest, Index, Vision Pro gaming)
- Major firmware updates that change the experience (handheld
  performance modes, console feature drops)

## Studios and series that almost always interest me
Surface their news even if the genre fit is borderline:
- Naughty Dog, Sony Santa Monica, Insomniac, Bend Studio, Sucker Punch
- Rockstar (GTA / RDR), Remedy (Alan Wake / Control / Max Payne)
- Kojima Productions (Death Stranding / OD)
- FromSoftware (genre fit borderline but cultural relevance)
- Capcom (Resident Evil borderline horror — skip unless Monster Hunter,
  Dragon's Dogma, Devil May Cry)
- Square Enix (Final Fantasy mainline, Triangle Strategy, Octopath)
- Bethesda (Starfield expansions, ES6, Fallout 5 — note the
  post-apocalyptic flag, but cultural moment beats it)
- Larian (BG3 follow-up)
- Annapurna Interactive, Devolver Digital (publisher fit for my taste)
- Housemarque, Asobo Studio, Playdead, Giant Sparrow, thatgamecompany
- Coffee Stain, Hello Games (No Man's Sky updates)
- 11 bit studios (Frostpunk too punishing — but This War of Mine, light)

## Sources to prioritize
**Primary news**
- Eurogamer
- Rock Paper Shotgun
- Polygon
- Game Informer
- The Verge gaming section
- IGN (less analysis, but breaking news; use sparingly)
- VGC (Video Games Chronicle) for industry scoops

**Reviews and aggregators**
- OpenCritic.com (more reliable than Metacritic for indies)
- Steam release notes and Steam Deck verification status

**Storefronts and platform feeds**
- PlayStation Blog, Xbox Wire, Nintendo of America news
- Steam News Hub, Steam Next Fest landing pages
- Epic Games Store free games announcements

**Insider / scoops**
- Jason Schreier (Bloomberg) — biggest single source for industry scoops
- Tom Henderson (Insider Gaming) — leaks (treat as rumor)
- Stephen Totilo (Game File newsletter)

**Indie discovery**
- itch.io trending
- Wholesome Games, Day of the Devs roundups

## What to skip
- Mobile games entirely (unless a major console game gets a mobile
  port worth knowing about)
- Esports tournament results, patch notes for competitive shooters
- Speedrun records
- Streamer / influencer drama
- "Top 10 most anticipated" listicles unless from a high-quality source
  with real reasoning
- Free-to-play live-service news (Fortnite, Apex Legends, etc.) unless
  there's a genuine event-level moment
- Visual novels (unless something exceptional like Slay the Princess)

## Format / tone
- Length: ~3 minutes spoken
- Style: narrator-led with Sam/Jordan riffing on the bigger releases.
  Sam is the curious one ("wait, what's the hook of this game?");
  Jordan is the "is this actually any good?" voice.
- Tone: enthusiast-friendly but skeptical of marketing copy. When a
  trailer is the only available info, say so. When a review embargo
  has lifted, lean on review scores.
