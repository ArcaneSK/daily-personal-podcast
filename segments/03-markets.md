# Markets & National News

## Why this segment exists
Brief me on what's happening in the U.S. government and the economy that
matters for my portfolio. I want signal that helps me understand what's
moving my positions and the macro backdrop, not generic "stocks finished
mixed" wrap-ups.

## My portfolio (use this to prioritize)
I hold these positions — anything that materially affects them is
relevant. Anything that doesn't move them is not, even if it's headline news.

- **AAPL** — Apple (single stock)
- **BOTZ** — Global X Robotics & Artificial Intelligence ETF (top holdings:
  NVDA, ABB, KION, Intuitive Surgical, Fanuc, SMCI, etc.)
- **CHAT** — Roundhill Generative AI & Technology ETF (top holdings: MSFT,
  NVDA, GOOGL, META, CRM, AVGO, etc.)
- **SMH** — VanEck Semiconductor ETF (top holdings: NVDA, TSM, AVGO, ASML,
  AMD, QCOM, INTC, etc.)
- **VT** — Vanguard Total World Stock (broad global equities — moves with
  global growth, recession risk, dollar strength)

So my exposure is: heavy on AI / semiconductors / megacap tech, plus
robotics, plus broad world. If a story moves any of those names or those
themes, surface it.

## What to cover
Cover anything notable in any of these areas. Prioritize portfolio impact.

### Single-name news
- **Apple (AAPL):** earnings, product launches, services growth, regulatory
  rulings (App Store / antitrust), supply chain news, China-related risk
- **NVDA / TSM / AVGO / ASML / AMD** (largest SMH + BOTZ + CHAT overlaps):
  earnings, design wins, capacity, export controls, regulatory action
- **MSFT / GOOGL / META / CRM** (CHAT overlaps): earnings, AI product
  shipments, regulatory action

### Macro and Fed
- FOMC meetings, Fed speakers, inflation prints (CPI, PCE), jobs reports
  (NFP, JOLTS), rate-cut probability shifts
- Treasury yields (10Y in particular), dollar index moves
- Recession signals, yield curve, credit spreads

### Government and policy
- Major federal legislation or executive orders affecting tech, AI, or semis
- Antitrust action against megacap tech (DOJ, FTC, EU)
- AI regulation (SB-style state bills, federal orders, EU AI Act
  enforcement)
- Trade policy: tariffs, export controls (especially U.S./China on chips,
  TSMC fab restrictions), CHIPS Act developments
- Debt ceiling, budget deal news that affects tech spending or treasuries

### Geopolitics with portfolio impact
- China / Taiwan tensions (TSM is critical infrastructure for SMH)
- Russia / Ukraine if it moves energy or commodities meaningfully
- Middle East if it moves oil meaningfully

### Sector and theme
- Robotics industry developments (BOTZ rationale)
- Semiconductor supply chain, foundry capacity, memory pricing
- Datacenter capex / hyperscaler buildouts (drives NVDA, AVGO)
- Big enterprise software shifts that move CHAT names

## Sources to prioritize
- **Wall Street Journal, Financial Times, Bloomberg, Reuters** for hard news
- **The Information** for tech-specific scoops
- **CNBC, MarketWatch** for daily market color (use sparingly — too much noise)
- **Federal Reserve, BLS, BEA** for primary macro releases
- **SEC filings** when there's a relevant 8-K or earnings release
- **Stratechery, Doomberg, Apricitas Economics** for analysis

## What to skip
- Generic "stocks finished higher/lower" daily wraps that don't move the names I hold
- Penny-stock pumps, meme-stock noise unless it actually relates to my positions
- Pure political news that doesn't have a clear market mechanism
- Crypto unless a megacap is materially exposed
- Bank earnings unless systemically important
- Fed-speak if it's just the consensus view re-stated

## Format / tone
- Length: ~3-4 minutes spoken
- Style: narrator-led with occasional Sam/Jordan break-ins on the bigger
  stories (e.g., a real Apple miss or a 5%+ NVDA move). For routine news
  the narrator can carry it solo.
- Tone: factual and tight, like a Bloomberg pre-market briefing — numbers
  when they matter (close prices, percentage moves, basis points on yields)

## Recurring threads to watch
- TSMC / Taiwan geopolitical risk premium
- AI capex sustainability — are hyperscalers still spending?
- Apple's services growth vs. iPhone unit growth
- Fed policy path (cuts vs. holds vs. hikes — the consensus shifts day to day)
- Antitrust trajectory against megacap tech
- Export-control regime evolution (especially semis to China)
