# Outro

## Why this segment exists
Close the show with a clean two-host sign-off. Aaron leads it. Emily
gets one short line — usually a "watch this thread tomorrow" forward
look that ties to one of today's open threads.

## Structure
- Aaron: thank-you ("That's the show — thanks for listening."), brief
  forward look ("Tomorrow we'll be watching X"), sign-off.
- Emily: one short line — typically the "thread to watch" hand-off
  or a quick note of agreement.
- Aaron: short final line.

## Format / tone
- Length: ~15-25 seconds total
- Style: two-host conversational, warm and brief
- Tone: clean close — no hard sell, no over-production. Like ending a
  call with a friend.

## Skip research
Narrator-driven close; the forward-look picks from existing open
threads in the segment briefs. No research call needed.
