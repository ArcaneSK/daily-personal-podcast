# Intro

## Why this segment exists
Open the show with a brief, warm greeting and a one-line preview of what's
ahead today. Keep it tight — under 30 seconds spoken.

## What to cover
- Acknowledge the date.
- One-sentence teaser of today's headlines (drawn from the other segments'
  research briefs — the script-writer can reference them).

## Format / tone
- Length: ~25 seconds spoken
- Style: narrator-only
- Tone: calm and present, not breathless

## Skip research
This segment is narrator-only and doesn't need its own news search; the
narrator can preview content based on the other segments' briefs at script
time. (Research will likely return empty, which is fine.)
