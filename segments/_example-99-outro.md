## Why this segment exists
Close the show with a clean two-host sign-off. host_a leads it,
host_b gets one short forward-look line that ties to one of today's
open threads.

## Structure
- host_a: thank-you ("That's the show — thanks for listening."),
  brief forward look ("Tomorrow we'll be watching X"), sign-off.
- host_b: one short line — typically a "thread to watch" hand-off
  or quick agreement.
- host_a: short final line.

## Format / tone
- Length: ~10-15 seconds total
- Style: two-host conversational, warm and brief
- Tone: clean close — no hard sell. Like ending a call with a friend.

## Skip research
Narrator-driven close; the forward-look picks from existing open
threads in the segment briefs. No research call needed.
